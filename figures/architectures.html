Inside the models

how each ingredient works on the graph — and how the hybrid combines them

KGAT — knowledge-graph attention

side information flows into the restaurant via attention

SAL — self-augmented temporal denoising

recent visits kept; one-off / out-of-character visits down-weighted

InfoNCE — contrastive learning

two noisy views of the graph; the same node is pulled together

Hybrid — InfoNCE-KGAT-SAL

all three mechanisms on one graph, summed into one objective

L is the training objective the model minimizes; λcl, λsal set how much the contrastive (CL) and temporal (SAL) terms count against the main ranking loss (BPR).
